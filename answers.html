Qlik Answers brings conversational AI directly to your analytics — no dashboards required. Type a question in plain English and get instant, context-aware answers from your data.

What is Qlik Answers?

Qlik Answers is an AI-powered, conversational analytics assistant embedded directly into your applications. It uses Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to pull accurate, source-grounded responses from your connected knowledge bases — so you always know where the answer came from.

  • 💬
    Natural Language Queries Ask questions the way you'd ask a colleague — no SQL, no filter menus, no training required.
  • 🔗
    Source-Grounded Responses Every answer links back to the underlying data source, so you can verify and explore further.
  • ⚡
    Instant Embed Drop a single <qlik-embed> tag into any page. No iframe wrangling.
  • 🔒
    Governed & Secure Answers respect your existing Qlik data governance, row-level security, and permissions.
